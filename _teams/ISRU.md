<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>ISRU</b> turns waste streams into functional nanomaterials at the point of need. 
By coupling cold plasma, selective extraction, and low-footprint synthesis, the team targets 
circular material flows for resource-constrained environments—terrestrial, field-deployed, and beyond.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: In-situ valorisation of heterogeneous waste streams into high-value nanomaterials.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>: Distributed, point-of-need conversion of heterogeneous waste streams into functional nanomaterials, 
enabling circular material flows in resource-constrained, remote, and field-deployed environments.</p>

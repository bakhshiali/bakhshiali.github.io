<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>SPIN</b> builds field-deployable nanofiber platforms for in situ fabrication and repair. 
Drawing on handheld electrospinning, nanofiber concentrators, and portable energy harvesting, 
the team moves nanofiber manufacturing from the benchtop to the point of use.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: Point-of-need nanofiber manufacturing.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>:  Point-of-need nanofiber manufacturing—portable, field-deployable systems 
that fabricate functional nanofibers in situ for repair, protection, and advanced drug release.</p>

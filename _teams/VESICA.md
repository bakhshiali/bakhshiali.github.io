<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>VESICA</b> engineers encapsulation architectures for controlled cargo release and targeted delivery. 
The work spans hydrogels, biopolymer carriers, and stimuli-responsive shells—designed for spatiotemporal 
precision in therapeutics, nutrition, and protective formulations.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: Spatiotemporal cargo control through programmable encapsulation.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>: Programmable encapsulation architectures that deliver precise 
spatiotemporal control of cargo release across therapeutics, regenerative nutrition, and protective formulations.</p>

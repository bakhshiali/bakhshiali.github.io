<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>PRINT</b> develops molecularly imprinted templates and recognition interfaces with antibody-like selectivity. 
The team combines polymer chemistry, ZIF/MIP architectures, and machine-learning-assisted screening to create 
robust synthetic receptors for diagnostics, separations, and targeted capture.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: Synthetic molecular recognition with programmable selectivity.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>:  Synthetic recognition platforms with antibody-like selectivity that can be programmed, 
manufactured, and deployed for diagnostics, separations, and targeted capture outside specialized laboratories.</p>

<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>ASRNL</b> explores the frontier of autonomous material systems: nanostructures designed to assemble, repair, 
and—over the long horizon—replicate under programmable conditions. The program integrates bioinspired self-assembly, 
stimuli-responsive chemistry, and closed-loop feedback to ask whether materials can become adaptive participants in their own fabrication.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: self-assembling nanostructures, feedback-controlled synthesis, and repair-enabling architectures.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>: Autonomous self-assembling and repairing nanomaterial systems that can adapt to their environment and sustain function with minimal external intervention.</p>

<h2>About</h2>
<p><b>NUTRIN</b> designs upcycled nano- and biomaterials for immune-supporting nutrition and regenerative outcomes. 
The focus is mechanistic: how material form, surface chemistry, and cargo presentation tune immune interactions and tissue repair.</p>
<p><b>Focus</b>: Immune-informed nanomaterial design for regenerative nutrition.</p>
<p><b>Long-term vision</b>:  Immune-informed, upcycled nano- and biomaterials that support regenerative nutrition and tissue repair 
through mechanistically defined material–immune interactions.</p>
